Reference intelligence

No references? It still starts from a field of taste.

Tastemaker now builds a local reference board before a major visual pass. It looks at category defaults, adjacent products, cultural sources, interface systems, and anti-references, then writes a direction contract the build must honor.

The full system

Every capability leaves a file, check, or visual artifact.

01

Read the work

Classifies the surface, audience, mode, density, and risk before touching the UI.

02

Generate tokens

Creates a fresh palette, then records safe color pairings in the style lock.

03

Show, do not tell

Turns product claims into screenshots, diagrams, charts, comparisons, and flows.

04

Source assets

Fetches or builds coherent icons, marks, favicons, illustrations, and photos.

05

Wire motion

Uses screen-specific tracks, reduced-motion fallbacks, and a motion audit.

06

Scan the tells

Runs `anti_slop_scan.py` and `audit_motion.py`, then fixes HIGH findings before handoff.

Anti-slop scan: no HIGH findings Motion audit: passed SVG assets: validated

Proof, not claims

The same prompt, before and after Tastemaker.

Persistent taste

The design decision survives the chat.

Project decisions stay inside `.tastemaker/`. Durable personal preferences promote into `~/.tastemaker/profile.md` only after real keep or reject evidence.

Optional registers

Same engine. Different visual languages.

Sponsor modes swap the type, shape, material, and motion dials on top of the same free core. The workflow stays the same.
